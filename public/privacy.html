Privacy Policy

Last updated: [add date when published]

This policy explains what information Vegas on Wheels collects through the GigStaffPro staffing app, why we collect it, and how it's used. It applies to workers who sign up, apply to events, or get assigned shifts through the app.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Name, phone number, emailTo create your account, contact you about shifts, and let you log in
A login PIN or passwordStored in encrypted (hashed) form — we never store or can see your actual PIN or password
Home addressOnly used to calculate mileage/travel pay for events near you — not shown to other workers
Skills, certifications, shirt sizeTo match you to events you're qualified for and order the right uniform size
Your location, at the moment you check in to a shiftTo confirm you're actually at the event venue when you clock in. We do not track your location at any other time.
Work history: shifts worked, hours, pay, and performance ratingsTo calculate what you're owed and track reliability over time
A profile photo (optional)Shown to admins for identification purposes

Who can see your information

Your contact information, pay history, and performance ratings are visible to Vegas on Wheels admin staff. Other workers cannot see your phone number, email, home address, or pay details.

Services we share information with

We use a small number of outside services to run the app. Each only receives the specific information it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase — hosts our database. All app data is stored here.
  • Google Maps — used to calculate driving distance between your address and an event venue, for mileage pay. Only addresses are sent, not your identity.
  • Resend — sends you shift invites, reminders, and confirmation emails.

We do not sell your information to anyone, or use it for advertising.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account and work history for as long as you're an active worker, and for a period after in case it's needed for pay or tax records. If you'd like your account removed, contact us using the information below.

Your choices

  • You can decline to share your location when checking in, though this may flag your check-in for manual review by an admin.
  • You can ask us to update or correct your information at any time.
  • You can ask us to deactivate your account.
